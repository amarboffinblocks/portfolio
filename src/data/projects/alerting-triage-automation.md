## Challenge

Incident alerts were noisy and manually triaged, leading to delayed acknowledgment and inconsistent escalation.

## Solution

We implemented rule-based triage automation with severity classification and team-specific routing policies.

## Outcomes

- Faster incident acknowledgment and ownership assignment.
- Reduced alert fatigue for operations teams.
- Better escalation discipline during peak load.

## KPIs

- Ack speed: +41%
- False escalation reduction: -24%
- Triage consistency: +31%

## Deliverables

- Automated alert triage engine
- Severity routing workflows
- Incident response visibility board
