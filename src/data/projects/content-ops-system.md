## Challenge

Publishing operations were inconsistent across teams, with duplicated effort and unclear review ownership.

## Solution

We built a content operations system with standardized templates, review workflows, and distribution-ready metadata.

## Outcomes

- Increased publishing cadence without quality drop.
- Reduced rework from inconsistent drafts.
- Improved cross-team editorial collaboration.

## KPIs

- Publishing cadence: 3x
- Rework reduction: -35%
- Editorial cycle time: -29%

## Deliverables

- Content workflow and template system
- Review and approval pipeline
- Distribution metadata framework
