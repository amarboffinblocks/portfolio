## Challenge

Service delivery teams lacked proactive visibility into blockers, dependencies, and workflow risk.

## Solution

We implemented a workflow intelligence layer that identifies delivery risk signals and recommends next-best actions.

## Outcomes

- Better on-time delivery reliability across accounts.
- Earlier detection of process bottlenecks.
- Clearer prioritization for delivery managers.

## KPIs

- On-time delivery: +29%
- Blocker detection lead time: +34%
- SLA variance: -22%

## Deliverables

- Workflow intelligence signal engine
- Next-action recommendation layer
- Delivery risk monitoring dashboard
