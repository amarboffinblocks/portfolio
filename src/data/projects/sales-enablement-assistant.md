## Challenge

Sales teams struggled to quickly access the right collateral, objection responses, and context during live conversations.

## Solution

We delivered a conversational assistant that surfaces relevant pitch assets and context-aware suggestions in real time.

## Outcomes

- Improved conversion through faster response quality.
- Reduced prep time before calls and demos.
- Increased consistency in sales narratives.

## KPIs

- Demo-to-proposal conversion: +27%
- Pre-call prep time: -36%
- Asset retrieval speed: +44%

## Deliverables

- Conversational sales assistant
- Asset retrieval and ranking logic
- Prompt and response quality governance
