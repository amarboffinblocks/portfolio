## Challenge

Inbound leads were manually reviewed across multiple tools, delaying response time and causing inconsistent qualification decisions.

## Solution

We implemented an AI-assisted qualification workflow that scores leads, enriches context, and routes high-priority opportunities automatically.

## Outcomes

- Reduced first-response delay for qualified leads.
- Standardized qualification across the sales team.
- Improved routing accuracy for priority accounts.

## KPIs

- Lead response speed: +31%
- Qualification consistency: +26%
- Manual triage effort: -43%

## Deliverables

- AI lead scoring workflow
- CRM routing automation
- Qualification audit dashboard
