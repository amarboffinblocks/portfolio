## Challenge

Finance approvals involved repetitive cross-team follow-ups and lacked clear audit visibility.

## Solution

We implemented multi-step approval workflows with policy checks, escalation logic, and exception handling.

## Outcomes

- Reduced approval delays across finance operations.
- Improved audit readiness and process traceability.
- Lowered manual coordination overhead.

## KPIs

- Bottleneck reduction: 63%
- Approval SLA adherence: +37%
- Audit trace completeness: 100%

## Deliverables

- Policy-aware approval engine
- Escalation and reminder automation
- Finance ops status tracking board
