## Challenge

Client onboarding required repeated follow-ups and manual status tracking, creating delays and poor visibility.

## Solution

We built a self-serve onboarding portal with guided steps, document collection, automated reminders, and operational handoff triggers.

## Outcomes

- Faster onboarding completion cycles.
- Fewer manual follow-up tasks for delivery teams.
- Improved onboarding transparency for clients.

## KPIs

- Ops effort reduction: 46%
- Onboarding completion speed: +34%
- Handoff errors: -29%

## Deliverables

- Guided onboarding portal
- Reminder and follow-up automation
- Team handoff orchestration
