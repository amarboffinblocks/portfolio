## Challenge

Client reporting was heavily manual, repetitive, and difficult to scale across multiple accounts.

## Solution

We built a self-serve reporting workspace with reusable templates, export-ready views, and configurable KPI modules.

## Outcomes

- Fewer ad-hoc reporting requests from clients.
- Faster turnaround for account reporting cycles.
- Higher clarity in stakeholder communication.

## KPIs

- Manual report requests: -52%
- Report turnaround speed: +35%
- Client visibility satisfaction: +30%

## Deliverables

- Self-serve reporting workspace
- Configurable KPI modules
- Export and sharing workflows
