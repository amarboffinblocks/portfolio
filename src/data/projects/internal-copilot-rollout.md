## Challenge

Delivery teams spent too much time on repetitive coordination, status lookups, and internal requests.

## Solution

We rolled out role-specific copilots with controlled context access and reusable task actions.

## Outcomes

- Higher delivery velocity across project teams.
- Better consistency in internal execution patterns.
- Reduced coordination overhead in daily workflows.

## KPIs

- Delivery velocity: +38%
- Internal request turnaround: +32%
- Context-switching reduction: -27%

## Deliverables

- Role-based internal copilots
- Reusable task automation actions
- Usage and quality monitoring dashboard
