## Key Takeaways

- Vague scope causes most automation failures.
- Missing ownership breaks reliability after launch.
- Monitoring is mandatory for sustainable automation.

## Mistake one: automating undefined processes

Teams often automate workflows before agreeing on the intended process.

This produces brittle automations that fail under real-world variation.

## Mistake two: no operational ownership

Automation without clear owners becomes stale quickly.

Assign responsibility for monitoring, exception handling, and improvement cycles from day one.

## Mistake three: shipping without observability

Without logs and workflow visibility, teams discover failures too late.

Instrumentation is what turns automation from a demo into a dependable system.
