## Key Takeaways

- Scale breaks manual workflows first.
- Standardization should come before automation.
- Ownership and documentation prevent process drift.

## Diagnosing manual bottlenecks

Manual systems usually break at handoffs, approvals, and status communication.

Audit these points first to uncover where delays and inconsistencies are compounding.

## Creating a scalable operating model

Define a shared process language, clear ownership, and success criteria before introducing tooling.

This foundation ensures automation reinforces the model instead of amplifying chaos.

## Sustaining operational quality

Use weekly process health checks to catch exceptions early and improve reliability.

Operational scale comes from repeatable habits, not just new software.
