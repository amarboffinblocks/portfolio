## Key Takeaways

- Adoption starts with workflow alignment.
- Rollout should happen in small, measurable slices.
- Fast feedback loops build trust in internal tools.

## Why adoption fails after launch

Most internal tools fail when teams feel they are adding steps instead of removing friction.

Adoption improves when the product is built around real daily tasks and handoffs.

## Rolling out with less resistance

Start with one team segment, define success metrics, and publish quick wins.

Visible progress encourages broader adoption and reduces change resistance.

## Making adoption a continuous outcome

Track usage behavior weekly and prioritize fixes that remove repeat friction.

Sustained adoption is driven by product iteration, not one-time onboarding.
