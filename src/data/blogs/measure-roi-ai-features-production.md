## Key Takeaways

- ROI requires both quality and efficiency metrics.
- Track feature impact at workflow level, not only aggregate usage.
- Instrumentation quality determines decision quality.

## Defining ROI for AI features

AI ROI is often misunderstood as only cost optimization.

A stronger model measures time savings, output quality, completion rate, and downstream business impact.

## Building practical measurement systems

Create a baseline before launch, then compare weekly performance by workflow segment.

This shows where AI is creating value and where guardrails need adjustment.

## Turning insight into iteration

Use performance and quality signals to prioritize roadmap decisions.

The teams that measure consistently improve faster and avoid expensive rework.
