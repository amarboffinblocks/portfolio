## Key Takeaways

- Choose stack based on team velocity and maintainability.
- Model integration should be isolated behind service boundaries.
- Observability is a core architecture requirement.

## Avoiding stack decisions based on hype

Teams often over-optimize for trends instead of delivery realities.

A good stack reduces cognitive load, supports iteration speed, and fits team skill depth.

## Designing AI layers for change

Models and providers evolve fast. Keep AI logic behind explicit interfaces with strict input and output contracts.

This makes swapping models and adding safeguards straightforward.

## Architecture for long-term reliability

Add tracing, usage logging, and error visibility from day one.

Reliable AI products come from disciplined engineering, not one-time model quality.
