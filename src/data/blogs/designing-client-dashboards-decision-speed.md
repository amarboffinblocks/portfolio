## Key Takeaways

- Dashboards should prioritize decisions, not data density.
- Information hierarchy drives speed and clarity.
- Contextual cues reduce interpretation errors.

## Why most dashboards feel noisy

Many dashboards are designed around available metrics rather than decision workflows.

When everything is equally prominent, teams struggle to identify what matters right now.

## Building decision-first layouts

Group insights by action: monitor, investigate, and resolve. Make high-risk signals visible first and move secondary metrics lower.

Decision-first layouts reduce mental load and improve response speed across teams.

## Improving usability over time

Run monthly dashboard reviews with real users to find blind spots and friction.

Iterative cleanup usually drives better outcomes than large redesigns.
